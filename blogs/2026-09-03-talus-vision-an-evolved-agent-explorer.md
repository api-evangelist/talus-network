Alongside the launch of Talus Protocol v2.0 on Mainnet, we are introducing a major overhaul to Talus Vision, transitioning it from a drag-and-drop agent builder into a specialized, agent-centric network explorer that you can use to verify the outputs of agentic work on Talus.
